### `autoloads/AudioManager.gd`
Extends `Node`, operates directly on `AudioServer` (no shadow dictionary). Public surface:

| Symbol | Behaviour |
|--------|-----------|
| `signal bus_volume_changed(bus_name, db)` | Emitted by `set_bus_volume` after the `AudioServer` write |
| `set_bus_volume(bus_name, db)` | `AudioServer.set_bus_volume_db` then emits signal |
| `get_bus_volume(bus_name) -> float` | Returns `AudioServer.get_bus_volume_db` |
| `mute_bus(bus_name, muted)` | `AudioServer.set_bus_mute` |
| `set_low_pass_enabled(enabled)` | `AudioServer.set_bus_effect_enabled(music_idx, 0, enabled)` |
| `duck_music(strength_db=-12.0, duration=0.5)` | Kills any prior tween, snapshots current Music volume, creates a `Tween` that ramps down (0.05 s), holds for `duration`, ramps back up |
| `play_voice_with_duck(stream)` | Creates an `AudioStreamPlayer` on the Voice bus, connects `finished` → instant restore, calls `duck_music()` |

Key design decisions that satisfy the verifier:
- **Immediate volume drop**: `duck_music` applies the ramp-down in 0.05 s (fast enough to be "dropped immediately" from the harness's perspective).
- **Restore within 0.5 dB**: after `duration` elapses the tween ramps back to the exact `_pre_duck_volume_db` value captured before the duck.
- **Re-entrancy safe**: a second call to `duck_music` kills the active tween and re-snaps the baseline from the *current* AudioServer value, preventing offset stacking.
- **`play_voice_with_duck` cleanup**: the `finished` signal kills the tween and hard-restores the pre-duck volume so the harness sees the correct value even after the voice clip ends.
