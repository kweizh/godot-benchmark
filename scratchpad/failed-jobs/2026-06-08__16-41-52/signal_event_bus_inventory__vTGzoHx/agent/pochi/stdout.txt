### Signal Contract (`EventBus.gd`)
- `item_picked_up(item_id: StringName, quantity: int)`
- `item_used(item_id: StringName)`
- `inventory_changed(snapshot: Dictionary)`
- `inventory_full()`

### Inventory Logic (`Inventory.gd`)
- `@export var max_slots: int = 8`
- Connects to `EventBus.item_picked_up` and `EventBus.item_used` in `_ready`
- Maintains a `Dictionary` of `item_id → quantity`
- Existing items stack (quantity added); new items require a free slot
- Emits `inventory_full()` and refuses the item when `_items.size() >= max_slots` and the item is new
- Emits `inventory_changed(snapshot)` (a duplicate of the internal dict) on every mutation
- `item_used` decrements quantity and removes the entry when it reaches 0

### Pickup (`Pickup.gd` + `Pickup.tscn`)
- `Area2D` with `@export var item_id: StringName` and `@export var quantity: int`
- On `body_entered`, checks `body.is_in_group("player")`, emits `EventBus.item_picked_up(item_id, quantity)`, and `queue_free`s itself

### Hotbar (`Hotbar.gd` + `Hotbar.tscn`)
- `Control` with 8 `Label` children named `Slot0` through `Slot7`
- Subscribes to `EventBus.inventory_changed`
- Renders each populated slot as `"<item_id> x<quantity>"`
- Empty slots are hidden via `hide()`

### Decoupling
Neither `Inventory.gd` nor `Pickup.gd` reference the `Inventory`, `Hotbar`, or `Pickup` class names — all communication flows exclusively through `EventBus` signals.
